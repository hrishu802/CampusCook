# 🍳 CampusCook – Smart Cooking & Recipe Platform for Students

**Built independently as a problem-solving project for campus life.**

---

## 🔗 Live Links

* 🌐 **Live App (Frontend):** https://campus-cook.vercel.app/
* ⚙️ **Backend API:** https://campuscook.onrender.com

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Students living in hostels or PGs often face:

* Limited cooking equipment (kettle, induction, etc.)
* Lack of easy and quick recipes
* Budget constraints
* Poor food choices affecting health

Most recipe platforms are not tailored for student life.

👉 This creates a need for a student-focused cooking solution.

---

## 💡 Solution

CampusCook provides:

* Easy-to-follow recipes for minimal setups
* Filtering based on available equipment
* Quick and affordable meal ideas

The platform makes cooking **simple, accessible, and practical**.

---

## ✨ Features

* 🔍 Search recipes by name or ingredients
* ⚙️ Filter recipes based on cooking equipment
* ⏱️ Quick and easy recipes
* 📖 Step-by-step cooking instructions
* 📱 Responsive and clean UI

---

## 🏗️ Tech Stack

## 📊 How It Works

1. User selects ingredients or equipment
2. Backend processes filters
3. Recipes are fetched and displayed
4. User follows steps to cook
